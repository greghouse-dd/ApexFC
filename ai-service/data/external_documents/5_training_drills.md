# Coaching & Training Exercises

## Pressing Drills

### Definition
Structured training drills designed to teach and refine collective pressing behaviors, pressing triggers, and defensive line coordination.

### Objective
To improve communication, physical speed, and spatial coordination when applying high pressure.

### Key Principles
- Press in blocks of players, never alone.
- Squeeze space relative to the ball's movement.
- Cut off central passing options using cover shadows.

### Player Responsibilities
- **Defenders**: Step up to squeeze the space behind the pressing line.
- **Midfielders**: Track deep runners and cover pivots.
- **Forwards**: Initiate the press, curving runs to direct the ball.

### Execution (Training Scenario)
- Play a 6v4 possession game in a 30x30 yard box.
- The 4 defenders must coordinate pressing runs to win the ball within 6 passes.
- If won, they score by transitioning to mini-goals.

### Advantages
- Develops collective team chemistry.
- Enhances aerobic and anaerobic conditioning.

### Coaching Points
- "Press as one unit!"
- "Don't let them turn!"

### Relationship With
- High Press, Pressing Triggers, Cover Shadow.

## Transition Drills (Transition Rondos)

### Definition
Exercises that focus on the moments of losing or gaining possession (transition phases), training immediate reactions.

### Objective
To train players to react within the first 3 seconds of a turnover (either to counter-press or to expand shape and counter-attack).

### Execution
- A 4v4+2 rondo in a grid.
- When the defending 4 win the ball, they must immediately pass to the wide neutrals to expand the pitch and score, while the losing team must counter-press within 5 seconds.

### Relationship With
- Attacking Transition, Gegenpressing, Rest Defence.

---

## Positional Games

### Definition
Training games designed within positional play concepts where players occupy specific zones representing their actual match positions.

### Objective
To teach vertical progression, spatial awareness, and passing through the "free man" in realistic match scenarios.

### Execution
- A 7v7+3 game on a gridded pitch.
- Players are restricted to their zones (e.g. wingers stay wide, midfielders in half-spaces) and must progress the ball through vertical lanes to score.

### Relationship With
- Positional Play, Half-Spaces, Positional Superiority.

---

## Rondos

### Definition
Classic possession keeping drills (e.g. 4v2, 5v2) where a circle of players passes the ball around central defenders.

### Objective
To develop close-control technical proficiency, quick decision making under pressure, and passing angles.

### Relationship With
- Numerical Superiority, Width.

---

## Wave Attacks

### Definition
Continuous transition attacks where teams attack in waves (e.g. 3v2 transitioning to 4v3, then 5v4) against defending lines.

### Objective
To teach rapid counter-attacking combinations and exploiting numerical advantages in the final third.

### Relationship With
- Attacking Transition, Counter-Attacking, Overloads.
